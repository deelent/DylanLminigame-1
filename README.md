# Minigame 1
## Devlog
1. I think a good metaphor for scenes, components, and gameobjects in Unity would be something like a lego set. A scene is essentially your baseplate, it's where everything happens in your game and often
encapsulate a specific moment, level, or cutscene, or in the case of legos, your building with all of your characters and mechanics inside. Your gameobjects are your minifigs, buildings, cars, etc. They are
The things that actually exist in your world, your baseplate, your scene. Finally, components are the individual pieces that make up the gameobject and therefore are comparable to the bricks that make up buildings
or the tires on a lego car that allow said car to move.
2. I wanted to actually talk about the dev process, I used coins to sort of steer the player in the right direction with the ultimate goal being a bunch of coins given there's no end level function. I don't know how
to move the starting camera yet and so I figured this was the best approach. I added a lot of verticality since I thought that would make it as visually intimidating as it could be but I actually slowed the player
down because, since it's a very tight-knit course, I wanted to make sure players couldn't skip over every object.
